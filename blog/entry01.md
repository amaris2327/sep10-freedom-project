# Entry 1
##### 25/10/2026

I've done a lot of thinking on what I want to be when I become an adult and what my interests truly were, and after a lot of thinking, I've narrowed it down to artistry/drawing. Now, for me, this was not exactly a difficult option to choose from considering that I've been drawing my whole life. I have folders full of drawings and comics, but that's all physical. What about digital art? How does something like that even come to fruition? Well, there are a lot of websites out there that allow you to make drawings and download them to your computer. One of my personal favorites is [Piskel](https://www.piskelapp.com/p/create/sprite/), a website that I've been using since 6th grade.
